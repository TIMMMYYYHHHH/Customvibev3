# Returns, cancellations and acknowledgements

23 October 2026. Owner-approved wording and manual operating policy. This patch changes only the relevant Terms and Returns copy, its dates and related TODOs. It introduces no order, payment, refund or email behaviour.

## Approved rules

- Cancel unpaid orders without charge. Refund a payment in full if cancellation occurs before making starts. Once personalised making starts, assess voluntary cancellation and any refund case by case, depending on whether work can be stopped. No automatic fee or percentage deduction is introduced.
- Standard catalogue goods retain applicable statutory return/cooling-off rights; no additional voluntary change-of-mind return period is offered. Do not label all catalogue packs or the game personalised merely because printed after purchase. Personalisation never removes applicable faulty, unsafe or misdescribed-goods rights.
- Customers self-preview in the designer/checkout. There is no separate artwork proof or order-acceptance step. Production starts once both order and confirmed payment are received, during working hours, normally Monday to Friday. No fixed production/dispatch time or public daily-capacity promise is added.
- Aim to acknowledge return, cancellation and complaint messages within **three working days**, then explain next steps after assessment. This is an acknowledgement target, not a resolution or refund deadline, and must not delay any applicable legal deadline.

## Manual handling

Use the existing orders@customvibe.co.za for returns/cancellations and hello@customvibe.co.za for complaints. Ask for the order reference, affected items, problem and requested outcome. Photos may help without becoming an unreasonable barrier. Check actual payment and making progress; an unpaid cancellation has no money to refund. Classify statutory remedies separately from voluntary cancellation and confirm the appropriate return/collection steps and costs. Do not require a separate acceptance event, restrict customers to store credit, or make contacting CustomVibe a restriction on statutory complaint routes.

No actual cancellation, refund, mailbox review, collection or production is claimed. The existing unpaid site and disabled payments remain unchanged. Status emails remain manual through Preview update and Send update.
